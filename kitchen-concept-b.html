Render Notes — Materials All materials should be rendered with PBR physical accuracy. Rosso Levanto must read as DEEP BURGUNDY-RED — not brown, not pink. Aged brass is unlacquered — warm yellow-gold with patina, NOT mirror polish, NO green oxidation. All brass hardware (knobs, pulls, tap, pot filler, pendant shades, range fittings) must read as ONE consistent material. Zellige tiles have strong variation tile-to-tile — NOT uniform. Grid lines and handmade surface irregularity must be visible.
